Ideas for Hendrik

This part is not on the website. It collects suggestions for getting from this mockup to a live site.

Launch order

  • Go live with this one page and the email sign-up first. Collect readers before the first devotional is ready.
  • Write four to six devotionals before launch so you never miss a week early on.
  • Pick one fixed day and time, for example Monday 5 am, in time for the dawn session.

Name, domain and socials

  • Check that wordonthewater.com (or .org) and the matching Instagram handle are free, and register both together.
  • Set up a domain email address (hello@, prayer@). It looks more trustworthy than a Gmail address.

Tools that suit a small ministry

  • Simplest: a newsletter platform like Substack, Ghost or Kit that gives you the site and the email list in one.
  • More control: Squarespace or WordPress for the site, plus an email tool for the devotional.
  • Many tools offer nonprofit or ministry discounts once you are a registered organisation.

Trust and accountability

  • Ask your pastor or an elder to read your statement of faith and the "Start here" wording.
  • Consider two or three trusted men who review devotionals before they go out.
  • Add a privacy policy before collecting emails or prayer requests.
  • Keep the ESV notice in the footer. Crossway allows quoting the ESV with that notice, within their limits.

Look and feel

  • Use your own surf photos rather than stock: early light, empty lineups, wax and boards. Real images feel honest.
  • Try the colour schemes in the bar at the top; the logo and every section follow along.
  • Make sure the site reads well on a phone. Most readers will open it from Instagram or email.

Later on

  • An online giving page, once there are costs to cover and a way to receive gifts properly.
  • Printable cards with a verse for the board bag or the car.
  • Local meetups: a dawn paddle out followed by coffee and a short Bible reading.
