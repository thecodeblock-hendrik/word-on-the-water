## The Lineup

Boxing Day is the busiest day of the year at Sarah's surf school. Half the town got a foam board for Christmas, and the beach is full of beginners. She starts every lesson on the sand: how to lie on the board, how to jump to your feet. But the talk isn't what gets people up. It's the moment she wades into the cold water beside a nervous learner, holds the board steady and says, "I'm right here. I'll tell you when."

Shouting from the beach doesn't get anyone up. Getting in the water does.

## The Word

Yesterday was Christmas. The wrapping paper is in the recycling and the leftovers are in the fridge. Today John helps us see who was really in that manger.

He doesn't start in Bethlehem. He starts before the beginning: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God" (John 1:1). A word is how you make yourself known, and Jesus is God making Himself known. "All things were made through him" (v3): every coastline, every wave, every person on the beach.

And He is light. "The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it" (v5). For four weeks of Advent we've waited in the dark for the light. Here it is.

Then comes the most surprising sentence ever written: "And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us" (v14). The God who made the sea didn't shout instructions from the shore. He got in the water. He became a real human being who got tired (John 4:6) and hungry, and wept at a friend's grave (John 11:35).

> The God who made the sea didn't shout instructions from the shore. He got in the water.

Not everyone wanted Him: "his own people did not receive him" (v11). "But to all who did receive him, who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God" (v12). Not a better mood for the holidays. A new family.

John was there. "We have seen his glory," he says, "full of grace and truth" (v14). He saw the same Jesus stand up in a boat and calm a storm, while His friends asked, "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?" (Mark 4:41). John's answer: He's the Word who made them, and He came to be with us.

## Paddle Out

Christmas is over, but He is still here. Use the quiet days this week to look again at who came. Read John 1:1–14 once a day, and each time you reach verse 14, stop and thank Jesus for coming all the way into your world. Then do what He did for one person who found Christmas hard: don't just send a message, turn up. A visit, a walk, a call.

## Pray

Lord Jesus, You are the Word who was with God and is God. Everything was made through You, even the sea. Thank You that You didn't stay on the shore, but became flesh and lived among us, full of grace and truth. I receive You. Shine Your light into my dark places, and help me turn up for others the way You turned up for me. Amen.

## Go Deeper

**Talk it over with a friend:** Who showed you what God is like by turning up, not just by talking? Who could you do that for this week?
