## The Lineup

There's a bloke at our break nobody films. He doesn't call waves or tell stories in the car park. But he's the one who drove four hours at night to tow a mate's van home. He sat with a grom's dad in the hospital after the reef got the boy. When the club's books came up short, he quietly paid the gap.

Ask him about any of it and he shrugs. "Had to be done."

No speeches. He just does the hard thing, even when it costs him.

## The Word

Joseph is the man in the nativity scene nobody looks at. Mary was promised to him in marriage, a bond so binding that ending it took a divorce. Then she was "found to be with child," and he knew it wasn't his.

Look at what Matthew says he planned. Joseph was "a just man and unwilling to put her to shame," so he "resolved to divorce her quietly" (Matthew 1:19). He wanted to do right, and he wanted to protect her. That's already a good man.

But God asked more. In a dream an angel said, "Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary as your wife." That was the costly part. People can count months. Taking Mary home meant sharing whatever the town whispered about her. His name would carry it too.

Here's how Matthew records his answer: "When Joseph woke from sleep, he did as the angel of the Lord commanded him" (v24). No argument. In fact, Joseph never says a single word anywhere in the Bible. His obedience does the talking.

> Joseph never says a word in the Bible. His obedience does the talking.

And he named the boy Jesus, which made Him, in law, a son of David. The name means "he will save his people from their sins" (v21). This child is "Immanuel… God with us" (v23).

Joseph carried shame for a while to welcome Him. Jesus would carry far more, "despising the shame" of the cross (Hebrews 12:2), to save men like us. That's the Christmas Joseph said yes to.

## Paddle Out

Is there one thing you know God is asking you to do that will cost you? Owning up to something. Making the apology. Stepping up at home. Paying back what you owe.

Do it this week, before Christmas Day. Do it quietly, without announcing it. Then tell one mate you've done it, so he can pray for you as it plays out.

## Pray

Lord Jesus, You are God with us. Thank You for carrying my shame on the cross. Give me Joseph's courage to do what You say, even when it costs me, and even when no one sees. Make my obedience louder than my words. Amen.

## Go Deeper

**Talk it over with a mate:** What's one thing you know you should do that you've been putting off because of what it will cost you?

**Read further:** All of Matthew 1, then Matthew 2:13–23 (Joseph obeys twice more) and Isaiah 7:14.
