G. Leaving the chat: three ways to say it will be cleared

Shown only when the thread has an answer or the message box has a draft; an empty chat leaves silently. A verse link never asks, because it keeps the chat. Close, Escape and the scrim use the same prompt as the bar buttons. Phones only.

OptionHow it worksForAgainst
1. DialogCentred dialog after tapping Devotional, Bible or Shore. Stay or Leave and clear.Clear, standard, nothing is lost by accidentExtra tap on every exit; interrupts a visitor who just wants to go
2. Bottom sheetSame question as a sheet rising over the bar, thumb-reachable.Easier one-handed; feels native on phonesStill an extra tap; covers the bar
3. Undo toastNo question. The chat leaves at once, and a toast on the next page says Chat cleared with Undo for 6 seconds.No extra tap; mistakes can be undoneThe thread must be held in memory for 6 seconds; the toast sits above the bar and must be announced (role="status")

H. Auto-hiding bar while reading (proposal)

On the devotional and the Bible reader the bar slides off the bottom when you scroll down, and slides back on the slightest scroll up. First the devotional and the Bible reader, the other pages after, the chat later. Phones only; desktop is unchanged.

WhenBarWhy
At the very top of the pageShownNothing to read past yet, and the bar is how a new visitor finds the other places
Scrolling down more than about 40pxSlides down and out (200ms)Gives the text the whole screen. 40px so a small wobble does not hide it
Any scroll upSlides back in (200ms)Softens the discoverability risk: a visitor who scrolls straight down finds the bar the moment they scroll back
At the end of the page or chapterShownThe end is where the next step belongs (Next chapter, the other places)
Focus moves into the barShownKeyboard and screen reader users must never land on something they cannot see. While hidden the bar is inert
Reduced motionNo slide, it just appears and disappearsprefers-reduced-motion
