Only the phone navigation changes (below lg). Routes, page content, header, chat and Bible reader behaviour stay as they are. Page content in these frames is placeholder text. Light is Dawn patrol, dark is Night swell, 375px. Every bar on this page is live: tap it, or hold and slide your finger or mouse across it. 11. Liquid motion

One pill, two springs. The edge facing the target moves faster than the trailing edge, so the pill stretches, thins a little, then settles with a small overshoot.

MomentWhat happens
Touch downThe pill blooms under the finger (or flows there from the current tab) and lifts to 106%. The icon grows to 114%.
Slide while heldThe pill follows from tab to tab, stretching. Nothing opens yet.
Release on a tabThat tab opens at once. Navigation never waits for the animation.
Release outside the barCancelled, nothing opens (WCAG 2.5.2). The pill returns to the current tab or fades.
KeyboardEnter or Space opens the tab and the pill flows to it. Focus has its own visible ring.
Reduced motionNo stretch or bounce: the pill appears in its new place.

12. Glass, readability and accessibility

Contrast is worked out here from the same values the bars use, against the worst possible backdrop: solid black behind the light bar, solid white behind the dark one. Blur only averages what is behind, so a real page cannot be worse than this. The saturation boost is not part of the sum and needs a check in a real browser.

SchemeLabel on glassLabel on the pillAccent text on the pill
  • So every label uses the ink colour. Selection shows as the pill, a filled icon and a heavier label, never as colour alone. Orange text on the pill fails in light mode.
  • Ask the Word loses its solid accent fill and Bible loses its raised circle: all three are equals, as in your picture.
  • Targets: the bar is 64px high and each tab is at least 90px wide at 320px, well over 44px.
  • Solid bar for reduced transparency, increased contrast and browsers without backdrop blur; system colours in forced-colours mode.
  • The bar floats 14px above the bottom, or above the home indicator (env(safe-area-inset-bottom)) where there is one. Pages keep bottom padding so the last line and the footer can scroll clear of it.
  • Web limit: this is frosted glass (blur, saturation, a light rim). Apple's native glass also bends the content at its edges. I have left that out because iPhone Safari cannot apply that distortion filter to a backdrop.
  • Desktop is unchanged, including the "Your chat" edge tab from lg.
  • Without JavaScript the bar never hides.
  • Hiding has a cost: someone who only scrolls down may not notice the bar until they scroll up. It is always there at the top and the end, and the header stays on screen.
  • Still to test on real phones: Safari's own toolbar collapsing during a scroll, and VoiceOver and TalkBack reaching the hidden bar.
  • Without JavaScript the links still work and the current tab still shows its pill (drawn with CSS), with no movement. Ask the Word stays a mail link.

13. Tablet

Yes: a tablet uses the new bar whenever the page is narrower than 1024px, which is where the current bar already shows. From 1024px it gets the desktop header and no bar, as today. The width decides, not the device.

Screen (CSS width)Navigation
iPad mini portrait (744), 11-inch iPad portrait (820)New bar, centred
Narrow split-screen window, landscape phone under 1024New bar, centred
13-inch iPad portrait (1032), iPad landscape (1133, 1180)Desktop header, no bar (unchanged)
  • One change for tablets: the capsule stops at 440px and is centred. Stretched to 800px, the buttons would sit far apart and the liquid pill would travel across the whole screen. The message pill and Undo toast use the same width, so the three line up.
  • It stays at the bottom, in the same place as on a phone: one component and one set of tests. A top tab bar would compete with the header, and a side rail would be a second navigation to build.
  • It needs no new breakpoint: a maximum width does it, and phones are unaffected.
  • As today, below 1024px the chat fills the screen and leaving it clears it. Hide on scroll works as on a phone. The passage picker stays a centred 544px sheet.
  • Rotating an iPad from portrait to landscape can swap the bar for the desktop header.

14. Decisions

  • Light glass for Dawn patrol and dark glass for Night swell, following the theme.
  • Chapter buttons use plain chevrons.
  • No resting pill: it stays only where a button is the current page.
  • The bar hides while scrolling down.
