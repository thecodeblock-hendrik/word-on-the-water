Prayer crew: suggested flow

Mockups for the prayer crew ([prayer_crew.md](../prayer_crew.md), ADR 0012). Phone first at 375px, light (Dawn patrol) with a few Night swell screens. Everything here is a suggestion for Hendrik to react to; nothing is built. The sections follow the journey: sign up and sign in, the signed-in menu, Prayer home, crews and invites, the invited person, writing and sending a request, receiving requests, destructive steps, the account, and a list of things worth adding.

1. Menu, sign up and sign in

One flow for both: enter an email, get a link. A new person is asked for a first name after the link, so the form never reveals whether an address is already registered. Prayer is not in the menu until you are signed in.

2. Menu for a signed-in person

Prayer appears only after sign-in, as asked, in the menu first. A badge shows what is new, because there are no notifications in version 1 and a crew goes quiet without some prompt.

3. Prayer home

The first screen after Prayer is tapped. Three states: nothing yet, an invite waiting, and an active person.

4. Create a crew and add members

Creating a crew is one field. Right after, the owner is taken to the invite step, because an empty crew is useless. Only the owner invites. An invite is a single-use link (7 days), sent by us by email or copied and sent by WhatsApp.

5. The invited person

Two paths from the same link: someone already registered, and someone who is not. The invite never shows more than the crew name and the inviter’s first name, and only to the holder of the link.

6. Create and send a prayer request

Written once, then shared with chosen people from every crew you belong to. A person in two of your crews is listed once. Sending sets who can see it; there is no email, and the badge in their menu is the prompt.

7. Receiving prayer requests

Everything shared with you in one list, newest first, with the first name of the sender and the crew it came through.

8. Leaving, removing and deleting

Every destructive step has a plain confirmation that says what will happen.

9. Account page and the night theme

The account page is small. Three screens are repeated in Night swell to check contrast.

10. Additions and decisions

Decided 2026-11-22 are marked Decided. V1 is recommended for the first release, Later can wait.

  • Decided, nobody ticked by default in the picker (6c). The button reads Save privately until someone is ticked.
  • Decided, “I prayed” tap in version 1 (7b, 7c). The author sees a count, never who. One small table: one row per request and person.
  • Decided, “new” badge in version 1 (2a, 3b, 3c, 7a). Needs a last-read time per person and request, so unread dots and counts are real.
  • Decided, report a problem goes to support@wordonthewater.net (4d, 7b). The address must be created first (a Squarespace forward, like hello@). Reports carry no prayer text.
  • V1, session expired and link expired screens (1f, 1g), and returning to the page you wanted after sign-in.
  • V1, crew limits and errors: crew full (5e), five crews reached, ten pending invites reached, an invite email that bounced.
  • V1, edit who sees a request afterwards (7d), including taking someone off it, and delete.
  • V1, rename and delete a crew (4c). Owner leaves: hand over or delete (8c).
  • V1, a first-time hint on Prayer home (3a) and the privacy line on sign-in and write (1b, 6a).
  • Later, notifications (Web Push or one optional weekly email) so people do not need to open the app.
  • Later, a prayer list of requests to keep praying for, an answered filter, and auto-delete after a chosen time.
  • Later, a signed-in navigation of its own, and a Prayer button in the phone bottom bar for signed-in people.
  • Later, Google sign-in button on 1b. Later, block or mute a member without leaving the crew.
  • Later, offline reading of requests (would need an encrypted cache, to keep the privacy stance).
  • Open, two accounts for one person (different emails): not solved in version 1; they are two people.
