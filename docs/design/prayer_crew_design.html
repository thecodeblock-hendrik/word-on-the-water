Mockups for the prayer crew (prayer_crew.md, ADR 0012), redrawn on 28 November 2026 for the live header and liquid glass bar, with Prayer added to both. Nothing is built. Names and requests are made up. Light is Dawn patrol, dark is Night swell. Phones are 375px wide. The glass has no liquid pill movement here: that is in landing_nav_new.html.

Tab widths

Each tab is as wide as its words plus an equal share of the spare room, so every label has the same space on both sides: "Ask the Word" is never crowded. Margins stay 16px at every width. Measured on the live bar with its own font, in Chromium and WebKit: 11.4px each side at 320px, 18.3px at 375px; the smallest tab (Bible, 320px) is 49.5px wide. The trade-off: widths differ, and on a devotional page, where Shore is shorter than Devotional, icons move by up to 15px (on Prayer pages by about 1px).

2. Hamburger menu and desktop header

Prayer sits after Bible, the same order as the bar. It is marked as the current page on /prayer and below, as Bible is today. Signed in, the menu adds who you are, Account and Sign out above Theme.

3. Welcome and sign in

Creating an account and signing in are the same steps, so the form never reveals whether an address is registered. After sign-in the person returns to the page or invite they came from.

Only the owner invites and removes. An invite works once and lasts 7 days.

Opening the link changes nothing: joining needs a button press. Before that, only the crew name and the inviter's first name are shown, to the holder of the link.

12. Rules for the build

  • Targets and type: every control at least 44px, inputs 16px or larger, visible labels, errors announced with aria-live.
  • Real controls: links, buttons, checkboxes in a fieldset for each crew, dialog behaviour from Radix. The Shared / Mine switch is two links.
  • "New" is never colour alone: it is a number or the word "New", and part of the accessible name.
  • Sign-in without a puzzle: paste works, the code box uses autocomplete="one-time-code" and the numeric keypad (WCAG 2.2, accessible authentication).
  • Nothing private leaks: no prayer text, crew name or person's name in a page title, address, analytics, Sentry or email subject. The one exception is the invite email, which names the crew and inviter. Prayer pages are no-store; signed-in views are not indexed.
  • Typing is never lost: a failed save keeps the text on the page and says what to do.
  • Motion and transparency: the bar's existing reduced motion, reduced transparency, contrast and forced-colour rules apply to four tabs.

Decided on 28 November (see prayer_crew.md, decisions 17 to 22)

  • 18 and over, confirmed with a box on the first-name screen (3f).
  • An emailed invite works only for that address (6f). A link the owner shares works for whoever uses it first.
  • Old shares never come back: when two people no longer share any crew, the access between them is deleted.
  • Signed out after 30 days without a visit, and always after 90 days.
  • Tabs are sized to their words, with 16px margins at every width (this replaces equal tabs, and the 10px margins first chosen for small phones).
  • Shore takes Prayer's place on Prayer pages. To be checked on a real phone.
