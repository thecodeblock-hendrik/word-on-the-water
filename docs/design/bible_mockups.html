Desktop and tablet selector: three options

For screens from 768px up. Mobile is not changed here. The problems with mockup 7: the chapter grid scrolls inside a tiny box, the verse picker is two steppers (many clicks to reach verse 16), and a single verse is awkward. All three options below let you choose one verse with one click, or a start and an end verse with two clicks, and all keep a "Whole chapter" choice. Controls stay 44px so they also work on a tablet.

Reading John 3:16–18 in the BSB

A. Columns. Everything is visible at once, left to right, so the order is obvious and nothing scrolls except the 66-book list, which you can also filter by typing. Chapters are a plain grid (no scroll box). Verses are a grid where the chosen range joins into one filled band, so a range reads as a range at a glance. Closest to what people already know from Bible apps; the safest option.

B. Type it (shown in Night swell). One text box. Type "john 3:16-18" or a short form such as "jn 3" and the best match appears with the verse text, so you see you got the right place before you read. Three matches are always offered: the exact range, the whole chapter and a single verse. Fastest for people who know the reference; slower for someone browsing, so the book and chapter list stays one click away from the same box (an empty box shows the books as a list).

C. In the text. There is no picker for verses at all: click a verse number to start, click another to extend (Shift-click works too, and a verse number is a real button so the keyboard works). The chosen verses are tinted with a bar beside them, and a small bar offers "Copy link", "Show only these" and "Clear". "John ▾" opens a short book list; chapters sit in a wrapped strip above the text (long books such as Psalms collapse the strip to "1 to 150" with a button that opens a tabbed grid). Most direct; the most different from what people expect, and it needs a short hint the first time.

Mobile selector: three options

For phones, below 1024px (tablets use these too). Same ideas as the desktop picker you chose: books with a filter and no counts, a chapter grid, a verse grid where the chosen verses join into one band, one tap for a verse and a second tap for a range, a Whole chapter button, and nothing applied until Read. Everything is thumb reach: the action bar is at the bottom and every control is at least 44px. These replace mockups 4 and 5 (the four-step full-screen sheet). The quick Bible switch (mockup 3) stays as the version chip.
