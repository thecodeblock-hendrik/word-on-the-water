Leaving the chat: five options for “Chat cleared”

Today a solid navy pill floats above the glass bar. Each option below makes the message belong to the bar. These are mockups only: nothing in the app has changed. Page text is placeholder. Light is Dawn patrol, dark is Night swell.

The same in all five: it appears only when there was an answer or a draft to lose, Undo is offered for 8 seconds, the countdown pauses while Undo has focus, screen readers are told “Chat cleared”, and a cleared chat is gone for good afterwards.

In each row the first phone is live: press Undo, or let the 8 seconds run out, then Show again. The other three are still pictures: dark mode, the Bible reader, and while the page is scrolling.

A. Glass twin smallest change

The same popup in the same place, made of the bar’s glass: same width, same edge and shine. Undo sits in a lens like the liquid pill, with a ring that empties over the 8 seconds.

  • Good: familiar, clear, works the same on every screen.
  • Watch: two glass shapes stacked cover the most page, and text behind glass is a little softer than on navy.

B. Droplet chip lightest

A small glass chip, only as wide as its words, floating over the middle of the bar. A thin line under it shrinks as the time runs out.

  • Good: covers the least page and feels like a passing note, not a second bar.
  • Watch: easier to miss, and Undo is a smaller target (still over 44px).

C. Bar shelf recommended

The glass bar grows taller and shows the message on its top shelf, then settles back. It is one piece of glass. All tabs keep working. While you scroll, the tabs fold away and only the message stays.

  • Good: looks most like part of the new bar, suits the liquid movement, covers a little less than today, and works the same on the Bible reader’s bar.
  • Watch: the most new behaviour to build, mainly the folding while scrolling.

D. Undo in the tab nothing floats

The Ask the Word tab turns into Undo chat with a countdown ring, under a tiny “Chat cleared” label. After 8 seconds it turns back.

  • Good: the quietest: Undo is where the chat lives.
  • Watch: for 8 seconds you cannot start a new chat from the bar. The Bible reader has no Ask the Word tab, so Undo would have to take Shore’s place there. The bar cannot hide while it holds Undo.

E. Side droplet most liquid

The bar narrows and a round glass button splits off to the right, holding Undo and its countdown ring. It merges back afterwards.

  • Good: playful, and close to the separate round button in newer iOS bars. All tabs stay.
  • Watch: every tab moves twice, so a tap can land on the wrong one. “Ask the Word” is already tight at 375 wide and does not fit at 320 (choose 320 above).

Recommendation

C, the bar shelf. It removes the separate popup that looks out of place, without taking a tab away or moving one sideways. If you want the quickest, lowest-risk change, take A.
