Cookie banner for the video pages

A and its wording approved 28 November. Drawings: No YouTube is loaded: "playing" is a drawing. Decided 28 November: a banner on the video pages only. The video pages are as approved in bible_project_advent.html.

A (chosen)BRules

A. In the page, above the videos chosen

It covers nothing: not Play, not the bottom bar, not the credit. It is sent with the page, so nothing jumps while the page loads. The cost: it pushes the videos down, so on a phone Play may not be fully in the first view. The page moves up when the visitor chooses.

B. Floating above the bar (not chosen)

The familiar look, and seen at once however far the page is scrolled. But on phones it covers Play until a choice is made, so in practice it asks for an answer before watching. A4 to A7 would work the same way.

Rules

  • Where: /videos and /videos/advent only, and only while videos are on.
  • Equal choice: Decline and Accept are the same size and style. Neither is pre-chosen. There is no close button that counts as a yes, and scrolling is not a yes.
  • Nothing loads from YouTube before Play, accepted or not. Accept only means that Play may load it.
  • Without Accept the pages, covers and "Watch on BibleProject" all work.
  • Remembered for six months in one cookie of our own, youtube-consent, holding only yes or no. Saving the choice is needed to respect it.
  • Changing it: "Cookie settings" in the footer of the video pages. After Decline, a video that is playing is removed.
  • Not a pop-up: it does not block the page or take focus. It is a named region near the top, so a screen reader meets it early. After a choice, focus moves to the page heading.
  • Without JavaScript there is no player, so the buttons only need to save the choice.
  • Privacy policy: it will describe the banner and the new cookie.
